Android Tech Lead | AI Tools Enthusiast

Hi, I'm Android Tech Lead at Bereke Bank, where I'm responsible for the business application. My professional interests lie at the intersection of engineering quality and team processes: I monitor codebase health, build horizontal connections within the team, and try to make the project comfortable and attractive for developers.

Since 2024, I've been experimenting with AI tools in everyday tech lead work — from auto-generating tests and documentation to offloading administrative and bureaucratic routines. I'm looking for what actually helps, without illusions about deus ex machina.

I write about various topics on my Telegram channel, co-organize nullptr.party meetups, and serve as an organizing committee member at GDG Almaty.
